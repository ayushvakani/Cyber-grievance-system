CERT-In Advisories on Cyber Security:
1. Phishing attacks: Users should not click on suspicious links in emails or SMS claiming to be from banks or delivery services.
2. Ransomware: Maintain offline backups of critical data. Keep software patched.
3. UPI Scams: Do not enter UPI PIN to receive money. PIN is only for sending money.
4. Social Engineering: Beware of callers pretending to be customer care executives asking for OTPs or screen sharing apps (like AnyDesk/TeamViewer).
