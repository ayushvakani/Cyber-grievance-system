SOP for Financial Fraud:
1. Immediately freeze the destination bank accounts.
2. Retrieve KYC details and call data records (CDR) of the suspect numbers.
3. Notify the nodal officer of the respective bank/wallet.
SOP for Social Media Harassment/Impersonation:
1. Preserve the profile URL and screenshots.
2. Send an emergency takedown request to the platform under Section 79 of the IT Act.
3. Request IP logs and creation details of the fake profile.
