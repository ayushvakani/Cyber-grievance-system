RBI Guidelines on unauthorized digital transactions:
1. Zero Liability of a Customer: A customer has zero liability if the unauthorized transaction occurs due to a contributory fraud/negligence/deficiency on the part of the bank, or a third-party breach where the customer notifies the bank within 3 working days.
2. Limited Liability: If the customer notifies within 4 to 7 working days, liability is capped depending on the account type.
3. The bank must credit the amount involved in the unauthorized electronic transaction to the customer's account within 10 working days of notification.
